# Beliefs

My current beliefs about the future:

1. Mainstream AI will remain human-centered.
2. AI safety and alignment will receive growing attention from both academia and industry.
3. Agentic AI will replace human workers in certain roles. However, new jobs will emerge around this new way of working.
4. The cost of tokens—and intelligence itself—will continue to fall, eventually making AI compute as ubiquitous and affordable as electricity.
