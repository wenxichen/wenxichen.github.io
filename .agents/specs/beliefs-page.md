## Goal

Give readers a concise, dedicated page describing Wenxi's current beliefs
about the future of AI.

## Decision

- Add a `Beliefs` navigation item immediately after `About`.
- Publish the page at `/beliefs/`.
- Present the four beliefs as a numbered list.
- Use the shared base layout so the page inherits the site's responsive
  navigation, typography, dark mode, and metadata.

## Content

The page covers human-centered mainstream AI, increased attention to AI safety
and alignment, labor displacement and job creation from agentic AI, and the
declining cost of tokens and intelligence.
